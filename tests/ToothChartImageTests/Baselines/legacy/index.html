Today's tooth chart, as recorded

The pictures of the DirectX, OpenGL and Simple 2D charts for every synthetic scene (package TC-0 of the WPF tooth chart plan). They are the "before" column of the owner galleries and the target of the parity probes. Under each picture: the mode that really drew it, the pixel format of the returned bitmap and its alpha, and for DirectX the adapter that drew it. Perio is drawn by DirectX only. DirectX was recorded without multisampling, so its edges are harder than on a computer that uses four samples: with them the same scene gave other pixels on every draw, and a later recording could not be compared.
